Las Vegas


Patricia visited Las Vegas, a lively city in Nevada known for its brightlights, famous hotels, and exciting attractions. She explored the Las Vegas Strip,watched the Bellagio fountains, and visited the shops along the busy streets.During her trip, Patricia also travelled outside the city to see the Mojave Desertand its beautiful rocky landscape. She enjoyed experiencing the contrast between thebusy city and the quiet desert. In the evenings, she admired the colourful lights andlively atmosphere of Las Vegas. By the end of her trip, Patricia had experienced someof the city's most famous attractions and made wonderful memories of her time in Las Vegas.
