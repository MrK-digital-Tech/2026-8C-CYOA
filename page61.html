AVALANCHE!!!

But it caught the men you were chasing?

Patricia sprinted through the snowy mountains, desperately trying to chase the men who are running away from her. Her feet slipped against the icy ground as she struggled to run faster. Suddenly, a loud cracking noise echoed through the mountains, followed by a deep rumbling sound. Patricia stopped for a second and turned around, only to see a massive avalanche rushing down the mountain towards them. She quickly threw herself behind a large rock as the snow came tumbling down, covering everything in its path. When the snow finally settled, Patricia slowly stood up and looked around. The men she was chasing were nowhere to be seen.

Patricia carefully made her way across the thick snow, trying to figure out what had happened. She spotted movement beneath a pile of snow and realised the men were trapped. She sighed, her breath showing in a large icy puff of air. She needed to go to the police- it was her only option to get safe and to get help from someone. Patricia trudged her way through the snow, making her way back towards civilisation with slow, careful steps.
