Other Arc de Triomphe in New Delhi

Patricia took a deep, steadying breath. She had been so certain he would be waiting at the crowded observation deck of the Eiffel Tower, parsing through the Parisian skyline with his pocket telescope. But after hours of scanning the sea of tourists and couples, she had to accept the truth: he wasn't there. Refusing to let disappointment dampen her resolve, Patricia remained fiercely dedicated to her quest, immediately planning her journey to the next logical landmark on his cryptic itinerary.


Her search brought her thousands of miles away to New Delhi, standing before the magnificent India Gate—the grand monument often described as India's own Arc de Triomphe. As she arrived, the sky bruised into a breathtaking, fiery sunset, casting long, golden shadows across the stone archway. It was a beautiful, humbling sight, yet as the last rays of light faded, she realized her uncle was still nowhere to be found. Unwilling to give up now, she pulled out his travel journal, searching for clues on where to look next.
