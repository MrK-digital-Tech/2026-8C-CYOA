At the Statue

Patricia had finally reached the towering Christ the Redeemer statue after following a trail of clues across Rio de Janeiro. As she stood beneath the famous landmark, she noticed a folded note tucked into a small gap near the statue's base. Curious, Patricia carefully unfolded it and discovered that it was signed by the statue's cleaner. The note read: "You've come farther than most. The next clue lies where few visitors think to look. Choose wisely." With the message in hand, Patricia faced a difficult decision. She could follow the cleaner's hint and make her way towards the statue's right hand in search of the next clue, or she could return to the hotel to rest, review everything she had learned so far,and perhaps uncover a hidden detail she had missed.
