A MOUNTAIN IN THE DISTANCE

You wait for a helicopter to take you somewhere safe. As you fly back, you look out the window and notice a mountain that matches the description in your uncle’s notebook. Looking closer, you spot a cave at the base of the mountain. Could this be where your uncle went? You have two choices: ask the pilot to land nearby so you can explore the cave, or continue to the airport and plan your next move.
