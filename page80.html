Severe or persistent vomiting can quickly transition from a minor stomach bug into a medical emergency. What begins as relentless nausea and retching can lead to severe dehydration, dangerous electrolyte imbalances, or warning signs like blood in the vomit from a tear or internal irritation. When the body can no longer keep any fluids down or is accompanied by sharp abdominal or chest pain, a trip to the hospital becomes necessary for intravenous (IV) fluids, diagnostic testing, and prompt medical care.
