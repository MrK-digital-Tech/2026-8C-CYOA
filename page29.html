Patricia at a hotel in Quebec

Patricia stepped into the grand, amber-lit lobby of the old hotel in Quebec, her suitcase wheels whispering across the polished floor.
The air smelled faintly of cedar and snow, and her heart thudded as she unfolded the slip of paper she’d found tucked into her uncle's journal. Room 314. She'd already followed him all the way here, and the number felt like a breadcrumb leading to something she wasn’t sure she was going to find.
The concierge, a woman with eyes like still water, waited patiently at the desk. Patricia’s fingers hovered over her phone, ready to book Room 314 online, but a knot of unease tightened in her chest. Was it better to slip quietly into his old room, to feel the echo of his presence without explanation, or to ask the concierge outright and risk hearing truths she might not be ready for?
The choice hung in the air like the first snowflake before it falls.
