To The Police Station

The Police


Patricia, a teenage girl, finds herself at the police station after a man in a black car stole her package when she inadvertently left it outside the school gate.However, her concerns seem to be dismissed by the officers, who prioritize contacting her parents instead of addressing her situation. Faced with the choice of exiting through the front or back door, she contemplates her next move.
