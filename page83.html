Patricia Slides into a Room

Patricia slides into the room. THUMP. She hits the hard, lumpy surface and opens her eyes. Before her stretched the Golden Room, its walls plated in gold leaf, its ceiling studded with gemstones that caught the light and scattered it in dazzling patterns. As she does, her eyes are illuminated with the striking colour of gold, reflecting all around. She picks up a nugget and feels the rough grooves on it. it's so heavy for it's size. She just stares at it for a moment, like she can't believe it's real.
She can't believe her eyes. She stood frozen, overwhelmed by the beauty and the weight of her uncle’s legacy. Every surface seemed to hum with history, as though the treasure itself remembered the hands that had hidden it. Patricia recalled his journal’s final words: “The gold is not for greed, but for the dreamer who dares to find it.” In that moment, she realized the room was more than wealth, it was a testament to curiosity, courage, and the bond they shared.
