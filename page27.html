A Chase Through Hawaii

The chase through Hawaii has begun! After everything that has happened — the dangerous journey, the close calls with the police,and the disaster that left you arrested and deported — you thoughtyour adventure was finally over.

But fate has other plans. Somehow, you have found yourself backin Hawaii, surrounded by swaying palm trees, crowded streets, and the distant roar of the ocean. Ahead, the tour bus disappears around a corner, and you spot something suspicious near the roadside: your uncle’s car, with the mysterious book still inside. Your heart pounds as you realise this could be your last chance to uncover the truth. Suddenly, you hear footsteps approaching behind you. There’s no time to think!

Do you dash towards the car and grab the book before anyone catches you, or do you follow the trail towards the hotel, hoping to discover where the bus has gone? Choose carefully — one wrong move could ruin everything.
