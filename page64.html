Uncle meets you and takes you to the border to meet Hill Tribe

Patricia was very excited to visit her Uncle Tom in Thailand. One morning, they packed their bags and travelled north through the beautiful mountains towards the border. Uncle Tom wanted Patricia to meet a hill tribe who lived in a small village. When they arrived, the villagers welcomed them with big smiles. Patricia met some children, learned a few words in their language and saw their colourful traditional clothes. The children showed her their village, their homes and how they made beautiful handmade crafts. Patricia loved learning about their culture and way of life.
As the sun began to set, Patricia thanked the villagers for their kindness. She had made new friends and experienced something very special. Uncle Tom was happy that Patricia had enjoyed the adventure. They said goodbye to the hill tribe and began their journey back home. Patricia looked out of the car window at the mountains and smiled. She would always remember her wonderful adventure in Thailand. The End.
