SUDDEN ERUPTION!!

Patricia, amidst her quest for clues about her grandfather, finds herself ascending a mountain in Hawaii when the ground beneath her begins to tremble ominously. The air fills with a low rumble, and she instinctively halts, her heart racing as she scans her surroundings for signs of danger.

As Patricia surveys her surroundings on the Hawaiian island, she hears a deep rumbling sound, signaling the imminent eruption of the volcano. Faced with a critical decision, she must choose between making her way to the base of the volcano for safety or waiting for a helicopter to arrive for evacuation.
