Cavern Filled With Gold

Patricia paddled her small boat through the dark underground river, following the directions in her uncle’s old notebook. Suddenly, the narrow passage opened into an enormous cavern filled with glittering gold coins, sparkling jewels and golden statues. She could hardly believe her eyes! As she reached for a handful of gold, her uncle’s notebook slipped from her bag and landed in the rushing water. Patricia watched in horror as the notebook floated towards a dark tunnel. It contained all \the clues she needed to find her way out of the underground cave system. Without it, she might never escape. However, the cavern was filled with enough treasure to make her rich for the rest of her life.
Patricia had to make a difficult decision. Should she paddle after the notebook and risk losing the precious gold, or should she stay in the cavern and collect as much treasure as she could carry? The river was getting faster, and she knew she could not waste time. She took a deep breath and looked towards the dark tunnel, then back at the mountain of gold. What would she choose? Her decision would change everything.
