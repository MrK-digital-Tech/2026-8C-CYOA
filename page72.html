New England Airport

The plane soared above the clouds, almost at its destination in New England USA.
"Passengers, please go back to your seats and put your seatbelt on, the plane is about to land. We hope you enjoyed your flight."
After the plane landed, Patrica grabed her carry-on bag from the over-head compartment and squeezed her way out of the plane and into the airport. The high ceiling loomed above her as people hurried around her. Patrica found a place to sit down and used her bag to make a make-shift pillow. She lay down and slowly drifted off to sleep...



"Hey, you! Wake up!"
Patrica bolted up, surrounded by the airport police, guns and batons attached to their belts.
"Uhm, do you speak English? Where are you from? Can we see your papers?"
Sweat beaded across Patrica's forehead. She had NO papers. If she got caught here now she would have no chance to find the answers she was looking for.
"Miss, papers, NOW."
Patrica's hands shook as she grabbed a hold on her bag.
"I- I don't have a visa..." She almost whispered, eyes held shut, scared of the cops surrounding her. Patrica gasped as one of them grabbed her firmly by her arm.
"I'm honestly supprised you got this far, but sorry, you're going home."
The officers threw Patrica into the back of a small, white plane, USA painted on the side of it. The cops slammed the door behind her, leaving her alone in the dimly lit cabin. The engine suddenly rattled on and Patrica wobbled her way into a seat, watching through the window as the airport got further and further away behind the clouds...
