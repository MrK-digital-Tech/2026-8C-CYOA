Come to a pontoon, Someone grabs notebook


 Patricia dipped her paddle into the dark water, steering her canoe carefully through the narrow underground river. The cave walls towered above her, their jagged surfaces glistening in the faint light of her torch. The sound of rushing water echoed through the enormous cavern, making it almost impossible to hear anything else. She glanced down at the notebook resting beside her, its pages filled with scribbled notes, strange symbols and clues that she hoped would finally lead her to her missing uncle. He had been gone for weeks, and every new discovery seemed to bring her one step closer to finding him.After what felt like hours of paddling, a wooden pontoon emerged from the darkness ahead. Patricia breathed a sigh of relief and guided her canoe towards it, eager to stretch her aching arms. However, just as she reached for the edge, a shadow darted out from behind a nearby rock. Before she could react, a hand snatched the notebook from beside her. "HEY!" she shouted, scrambling out of the canoe as the mysterious figure sprinted towards a narrow passageway. Without a second thought, Patricia launched herself after him, her footsteps splashing through the shallow water.
The passage gradually sloped upwards, and the damp cave walls slowly gave way to rough stone steps. Patricia's lungs burned as she raced after the thief, desperately trying to keep his dark figure in sight. At last, a blinding shaft of daylight appeared ahead. She burst through the opening and stumbled onto a cobbled street in a small, unfamiliar town. The sudden brightness made her squint, and by the time her eyes had adjusted, the thief had disappeared into the bustling crowd. She spun around, searching desperately between the passing pedestrians, but there was no sign of him. Her uncle's notebook was gone.Patricia's eyes darted around the street, searching for anything that might help her catch up. Then she spotted two possibilities. A bicycle leaned against a nearby shopfront, its handlebars slightly crooked but seemingly in working condition. Further down the road, a taxi sat waiting beside the pavement, its driver tapping impatiently on the steering wheel. She hesitated, her mind racing. The bicycle would let her weave through the narrow streets, but the taxi might be faster over a longer distance. She couldn't afford to waste another second.
