The Notebook

After grabbing her uncles notebook from the huge pile of gold, Patrica ran outside, clutching the notebook tightly in her arms. When she finally got to the outside of the cave, she stopped to catch her breath. Patrica sat down at the mouth of the cavern, about to open the notebook she had spent so much time chasing, when SWOOP! A masked theif snatched the notebook out of Patrica's trembling hands.
"Thanks for doing all the work for me! I'll be taking this now." The person said as they ran off into the nearby crowd.


Patrica stood there shaking from shock. She started running after them but as soon as she reached the crowd they had run into, there was no trace of them. She knew she had a limited time to make a choice about what she was going to to catch this mystery theif. Patrica had two options: chase them on one of the rental e-bikes nearby, or call a taxi to chase them down. Time was running out. She had to choose one soon or Patrica might never see her uncles journal again.
