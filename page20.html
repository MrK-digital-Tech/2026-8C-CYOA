A cave in Hawaii


Patricia’s canoe drifted quietly through the cool, echoing cave, the only sound the gentle tap of water against the hull. Her torchlight danced across the ancient walls, revealing textures carved by centuries of tides. When she leaned over the side to get a better look at the shimmering water below, the torch slipped from her hand. It tumbled into the dark pool with a soft hiss, sinking just far enough to illuminate something unexpected—an underwater passage, glowing faintly as if inviting her in.

She steadied her breath, gripping the paddle as she considered what to do next. The submerged tunnel promised mystery, maybe even discovery, but it also meant diving into cold, unknown depths. Behind her, the stream continued onward, carrying a soft green glow that pulsed like a heartbeat deeper in the cave. Two paths, each with its own risks and wonders, waited for her to choose.
