The Dark Corridor

After following the clues left behind by your uncle, you finally discover a hidden entrance inside an ancient Egyptian pyramid. You step through the stone doorway and find yourself in a long, dark corridor. The air is cold, and the walls are covered in strange symbols that seem to match the drawings in your uncle's notebook. You switch on your torch and begin walking deeper into the pyramid. Suddenly, you hear a loud rumble behind you. The entrance has sealed shut! After several minutes, you reach a fork in the passage. On the left, a golden inscription reads TREASURE, and you can see a faint golden glow coming from beneath the doorway. On the right, a weathered stone sign reads HARD WORK, with a narrow staircase disappearing into darkness. You remember something your uncle once told you: "The easiest path is not always the path worth taking."




You take a deep breath. Your torch is running low, and you need to make a decision quickly. Which path will you choose?

Choice 1: Treasure Choice 2: Hard Work
