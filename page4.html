At the Airport

Patricia rushed through the airport, weaving between people as she tried to keep up with the mysterious figures ahead of her. Her heart pounded as she reached the departure gates, where two flights were about to board. One was heading to Canada, and the other was flying to Hawaii. She glanced between the two gates, unsure which way the people had gone. There was barely any time left,and she knew she had to make a decision before they disappeared completely.

She hurried over to the departure screens and studied the details of both flights. Canada was cold and covered in snow, with huge forests and mountains that could hide all sorts of secrets. Hawaii was warm and tropical, with beautiful beaches and busy streets where anyone could blend into the crowd.Patricia tightened her grip on her ticket and took a deep breath. She had two choices, but only one chance to find the people she was chasing. Would she boardthe plane to Canada or take the flight to Hawaii?
