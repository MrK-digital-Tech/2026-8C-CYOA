Showing the Notebook



Patricia is a teenage girl of Pakistani heritage who lives in Australia. She is standing outside the school gates with her friend, holding her uncle’s notebook carefully in her hands. Curious and excited, she opens the notebook to show her friend what is inside. The two girls lean closer together, looking through its pages and wondering about the stories, memories and secrets it might hold. Patricia feels a special connection to her uncle as she shares something that is important to her, while the busy school grounds surround them.


Patricia’s friend began staring at the notebook with greedy eyes, her curiosity slowly turning into jealousy. As Patricia continued explaining the strange things written on its pages, her friend edged closer, keeping her eyes fixed on the notebook. Suddenly, without warning, she snatched it from Patricia’s hands and took off running. Patricia gasped in shock, realising what had happened. “Hey! Give that back!” she shouted, rushing after her friend as the notebook disappeared into the distance.
