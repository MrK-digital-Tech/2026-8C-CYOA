The Gold is Too Heavy!

The moment you heave the dense nugget into your arms, a strange sensation washes over you—the fine, crystalline sand beneath your boots begins to give way, behaving more like water than solid ground. You aren't trapped or stuck, but your increased weight is causing you to slowly, steadily sink deeper into the cavern floor with every passing second. Looking back toward the entry tunnel, you remember your discarded leather notebook, while ahead, the mysterious sandy path stretches onward out to the sandy beach.
Which will you choose?
