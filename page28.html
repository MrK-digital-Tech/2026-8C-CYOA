OH NO! THEY CAUGHT UP TO YOU!


Patricia stood at the curb, contemplating her next move before she decided to hail a taxi. As the vehicle approached, she stepped forward, opened the door, and slid into the back seat, providing the driver with her destination. The taxi pulled away smoothly, merging into the flow of traffic. As they navigated through the bustling streets, the sudden wail of a police siren pierced the air, causing her to glance out the window, curious about the commotion unfolding nearby.

Patricia finds herself in a tense situation as the police pull over the taxi she is riding in. Despite the seriousness of the moment, she remains uncooperative, leading to a chaotic police chase. Ultimately, the authorities manage to stop the taxi, and Patricia faces the consequences of her actions, resulting in her deportation.
