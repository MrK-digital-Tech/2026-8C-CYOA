Tuk Tuk Driver Knows Uncle

The tuk-tuk rattled through the busy streets of Bangkok, weaving between cars and motorbikes as Patricia held tightly onto the side. Her long brown hair was tucked beneath her yellow headscarf , and her blue shirt fluttered in the warm breeze. Suddenly, the driver glanced at her through the mirror. “You looking for someone?” he asked. Patricia quickly explained that she was searching for her Uncle, hoping he could help her continue her adventure.

The driver’s eyes widened. “Your Uncle? I know him! He told me you might be coming.” Patricia leaned forward in surprise. Maybe she had finally found someone who could help her. The driver turned down a narrow street, leaving the noisy traffic behind. “Hold on,” he said with a mysterious smile. “I’ll take you straight to him.” Patricia wasn’t sure whether to trust him, but she had come too far to turn back now.
