At the cave

After days of hiking through forests, crossing rivers, and following the faded treasure map, you finally arrive at the cave marked with a giant red X. A volcano looms nearby, its rocky slopes stretching into the clouds. The cave entrance is half-filled with dark water. An old canoe is tied to a wooden post inside. Steam drifts from cracks in the ground near the volcano.

You have two choices: A. Climb into the canoe and paddle into the cave. B. Walk towards the bottom of the volcano.
