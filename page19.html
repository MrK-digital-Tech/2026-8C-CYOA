A Hidden Message!

Patricia stepped into the Louvre Museum in Paris, France, feeling nervous but excited about what she might discover. The museum was enormous, with hundreds of paintings and sculptures displayed along its long corridors. She followed the signs until she reached a crowded room where the famous Mona Lisa hung behind protective glass. Pushing her way through the crowd, Patricia finally managed to get a clear view of the painting. Something unusual caught her eye near the edge of the frame. She leaned slightly to the side and noticed tiny letters carved into the wood, almost impossible to see unless you knew where to look.

Patricia’s eyes widened as she realised the letters formed a hidden message. She quickly glanced around, hoping nobody else had noticed it. The message seemed important, and she knew she needed to keep a record of it before moving on. However, the museum was crowded, and a security guard was slowly making his way around the room. Patricia had two options: she could take a quick photo of the message and risk attracting attention, or move closer to the frame and carefully write the letters down in her notebook. She only had a few moments to decide before the guard reached her.
