Patrica meets Mountie

Patricia’s heart leaped with unexpected joy when she crossed paths with Mountie, an old friend she hadn't seen in years, their chance reunion instantly dissolving the decades between them. After exchanging warm embraces and catching up on lost time, Mountie gently guided Patricia toward the edge of the old river bridge to show her something important.Pointing across the weathered wooden structure, Mountie revealed that he had recently stood right there and watched her uncle walkacross to the other side.

He explained that he simply saw her uncle cross the bridge and continue on his way down the path until he disappeared from view. With the mystery of where her uncle had gone finally cleared up, the two friends smiled, shared one last warm embrace, and said their goodbyes before walking away in opposite directions. She crossed the bridge, wondering where she should stop next.
