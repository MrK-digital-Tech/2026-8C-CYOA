Introduction


Patricia sat at the kitchen table eating breakfast, enjoying a quiet Friday morning before going to school. She was halfway through her cereal when she heard a loud knock at the front door.Patricia looked up in surprise. She wasn't expecting anyone.When she opened the door, a delivery driver handed her a small, carefully wrapped package. There was no return address, but her name was written clearly on the front.Patricia's eyes widened when she saw who had sent it. The package was from her famous Uncle George, an adventurer who travelled the world and was always getting into mysterious situations.
Patricia carried the package back to the kitchen and placed it on the table. She noticed that it was unusually heavy for its size. A note tucked under the string simply said, "Do not open this until you are ready for an adventure." Patricia stared at the package. Curiosity overcame her and she hurridely opened it to reveal her Uncle's notebook. She had always seen it in his hands or in his satchel but he never let it out of his sight. What had happened to him? She decided to take the notebook to school to read before deciding what to do next.
