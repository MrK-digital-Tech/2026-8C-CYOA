The Massive Earthquake

Patricia was walking through the city, enjoying the beautiful day, when suddenly the ground began to shake violently. A massive earthquake had struck! Buildings trembled, windows shattered and people screamed as they rushed to find safety. Patricia quickly ran towards a nearby museum for shelter. Once the shaking stopped, she noticed something unusual buried beneath the broken floor and fallen concrete. Curious, she moved closer and discovered a magnificent ancient Māori artefact hidden beneath the rubble.

It was a beautifully carved wooden treasure decorated with intricate traditional patterns. Realising how important it could be to Māori history, Patricia alerted the museum staff and emergency workers. The experts carefully recovered the artefact and examined it. Patricia was delighted when the museum decided to preserve the discovery and display it so visitors could learn about Māori culture and history. Although the earthquake had been terrifying, Patricia had helped uncover something extraordinary that would be remembered for generations.
