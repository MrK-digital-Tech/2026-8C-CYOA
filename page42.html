A Puzzle in the Sphinx

Patricia stepped through a narrow doorway into a hidden room deep inside the ancient sphinx structure in the desert. The room was dimly lit by flickering torches, and the walls were covered in strange symbols and faded paintings. In the centre stood a large stone table with a complicated puzzle carved into its surface. Intrigued, she studied the puzzle carefully, moving the stone tiles one by one while dust drifted from the ceiling. Every move seemed to bring her closer to completing it.

After nearly an hour, Patricia reached the final stage of the puzzle. All the tiles were in place except for one, and two arrows had appeared on the stone table, pointing in opposite directions: left and right. A faint clicking sound echoed through the room as Patricia examined the final tile. Patricia looked between the two options, trying to decide which path to take. With the final move still unfinished, she stood silently before the puzzle, knowing that whichever direction she chose would determine what happened next.
