Skiing after suspicious men..

The wind howled across the mountain as 15-year-old Patricia carved through the snow on her skis. Ahead, a group of suspicious men in dark jackets raced down the slope, occasionally looking over their shoulders as if they were hiding something. Patricia had spotted them sneaking around a restricted research cabin earlier that morning, and when they fled, she followed. Now the gap between them was shrinking. Snow sprayed into the air as she pushed herself faster, her heart pounding against her ribs. Just ahead, the men disappeared around a rocky ridge, and Ava spotted two possible routes to catch them.

To her left was a narrow path that ducked beneath a giant snow overhang. It was much quicker and could cut off the men before they reached the next valley, but the heavy shelf of snow looked unstable. One loud sound could bring it crashing down. To her right was a steep climb over the mountain ridge. It was safer, with solid ground and a clear view of the area beyond, but it would take longer and the men might escape. Patricia tightened her grip on her ski poles and took a deep breath. Will she ski under the snow overhang and risk an avalanche to gain speed? Or will she climb over the mountain and play it safe, even if it means losing precious time? Choice A: Chase the men under the snow overhang (quicker, but dangerous). Choice B: Climb over the mountain ridge (slower, but safer).
