Room has hidden compartment


 Patricia stepped into the hotel, her eyes scanning the grand entrance as she approached the reception desk. She had spent so long searching for her missing uncle, following every tiny lead and chasing every rumour, and now she had finally arrived at the very hotel where he had last been seen. Trying to hide her nervousness, she gave the receptionist a polite smile and asked if there were any rooms available. When the receptionist asked which room she would prefer, she hesitated before quietly requesting the exact same room her uncle had booked during his stay. The receptionist gave her a curious look but said nothing, handing over a small golden key attached to a wooden keyring.After making her way up the creaking staircase, she stopped outside the familiar room number. Her heart began to pound as she slid the key into the lock and slowly pushed the door open. The room was surprisingly ordinary, with faded wallpaper, heavy curtains and a large wooden wardrobe standing against one wall. She stepped inside, closing the door firmly behind her. If her uncle had been here, he must have left something behind. Something that could explain where he had gone. She began carefully searching the room, checking the drawers, looking beneath the bed and inspecting the dusty bedside table. She even ran her fingers along the edges of the paintings, searching for anything that seemed out of place.
Just as she was beginning to lose hope, she noticed something peculiar about the wardrobe. It stood slightly away from the wall, leaving a narrow gap behind it. Frowning, she dragged it forward with a strained grunt, revealing a small, rectangular panel hidden in the wall. She crouched down, brushing away the thick layer of dust that covered its edges. There, barely visible beneath the grime, were several tiny screw holes. Her breath caught. This wasn't just a loose piece of wallpaper or a damaged section of plaster. It was a secret compartment.She leaned closer, examining the panel. There were two possible ways to get inside. She could find something heavy and break through the thin wooden covering, although that would undoubtedly make a great deal of noise and leave a mess. Or, she could search the room for a screwdriver and carefully unscrew the panel, preserving whatever might be hidden inside. She glanced towards the door, then back at the mysterious compartment. Whatever her uncle had left behind could be inside. But which option should she choose?
