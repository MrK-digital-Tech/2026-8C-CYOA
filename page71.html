Swim To Yacht

After spending weeks exploring a remote tropical island, Patricia notices a magnificent luxury yacht sailing across the ocean. She suddenly remembers that her uncle had promised her an unforgettable surprise: an all-expenses-paid cruise around the world! Excited by the opportunity, Patricia realises that the yacht is the cruise ship her uncle has booked for her. She quickly swims towards it, hoping to catch up before it sails away. Once she reaches the yacht, the crew help her aboard and welcome her to the incredible adventure. With the world waiting to be explored, Patricia prepares to visit some of the most famous destinations on Earth. But where will her journey take her first?
