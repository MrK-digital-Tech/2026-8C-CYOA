Room turns into slide!


Patricia dragged herself along the seemingly endless corridor, her feet aching with every step. She was beginning to wonder whether this place would EVER end when she noticed something strange beneath her feet. The smooth corridor floor had started curving downwards, gradually transforming into an enormous slide! Patricia blinked in disbelief, leaning forward to inspect the bizarre discovery. "You've GOT to be kidding me," she muttered, staring down the slippery slope that disappeared around a corner.










SLIDE DOWN FINISH! She glanced behind her, hoping there might be another way forward, but the corridor stretched endlessly into the distance. Ahead, the mysterious slide looked surprisingly smooth, although she had absolutely no idea where it led. Patricia sighed dramatically, brushing a strand of hair from her face. She could either keep dragging herself along the corridor and finish her exhausting journey, or take a completely unexpected shortcut and slide down into whatever awaited her below. What should Patricia do?
