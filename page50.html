Patricia Follows Man with Uncle's Hat

Patricia spotted the familiar hat first—a faded brown brim with a tiny tear on the left side, unmistakably her uncle’s. The man wearing it walked briskly down the footpath, glancing over his shoulder as though checking whether anyone noticed. Patricia followed at a careful distance, her curiosity rising with every step. When he reached the corner, a car pulled up, and to her surprise, her uncle stepped out, greeting the man as if they had planned the meeting all along. Patricia froze, unsure what to make of the scene, but certain she needed to know more.
She hurried toward the street, only to find the car already pulling away. At the curb beside her stood a bike, leaning against a post, and just beyond it a taxi idled, its driver tapping the steering wheel impatiently. Patricia felt the weight of the moment settle on her shoulders. The bike meant independence and speed through unfamiliar back streets; the taxi meant a chance to catch up quickly but at the mercy of someone else’s route.

With the car disappearing into the distance, she took a breath and made her choice, knowing it would shape whatever she discovered next.
