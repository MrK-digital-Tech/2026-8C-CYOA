room full of gas

Patricia stood in the middle of the dim room, she turned toward the sound, confusion tightening her brow as a thin stream of gas began to spill from a vent on the wall. The pale cloud curled around her shoes first, rising slowly, unnervingly, as the air grew heavy and chemical.

Her heartbeat quickened. Patricia took a step back, then another, her curls bouncing as she scanned the room for any sign of escape. The gas thickened, swirling like fog, brushing against her legs and climbing higher. She pressed a hand to the wall, steadying herself, knowing she had only moments before the room filled completely. The ordinary space around her had transformed into something dangerous, and every instinct told her she needed to move—now.
